✦ MEET YOUR HOST ✦

A sly little fox with an excellent poker face.

Blackjack has a reputation for a little chaos, a fondness for magic tricks, and jokes so bad they somehow circle back around to being funny. Perhaps the real trick is getting the audience to laugh before they notice their card has disappeared.

Among the Dream Drops Crew, the fox belongs to the same enchanted world as the forest's spirits and the stories that grow from unexpected encounters. His tale has its darker corners, too — but tonight, the spotlight is on laughter.
